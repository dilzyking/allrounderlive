Spain vs France – World Cup Semi-Final

📅 14 July 2026 📍 Allianz Arena, Munich ⏱️ 21:00 IST 🏆 FIFA World Cup 2026 – Semi-Final

Spain and France face off in a blockbuster World Cup semi-final at the Allianz Arena in Munich. Two of Europe's footballing giants collide with a place in the final on the line. Spain's tiki-taka mastery against France's athleticism and flair promises a captivating encounter.

Spain's Road to the Semi-Finals

Luis de la Fuente's Spain have been the tournament's most impressive side, winning all five matches with a goal difference of +12. Their quarter-final demolition of Belgium (3-0) showcased their attacking fluidity, with Lamine Yamal and Nico Williams tearing apart opposition defences. The midfield trio of Rodri, Pedri, and Gavi has controlled every game, while the defence has conceded just two goals.

France's Journey

Didier Deschamps' France have been resilient and clinical. After a shaky group stage, they've grown into the tournament, beating Portugal 2-1 in the quarter-finals. Kylian Mbappé has been electric with 5 goals, while Antoine Griezmann has provided the creative spark. The midfield duo of Aurélien Tchouaméni and Adrien Rabiot has provided the perfect balance of steel and creativity.

Tactical Breakdown

Spain will look to dominate possession with their trademark passing game, using the width provided by Yamal and Williams to stretch the French defence. France, meanwhile, will sit deep and look to hit on the counter through the pace of Mbappé and Dembélé. The battle between Rodri and Tchouaméni in midfield will be pivotal.

Head-to-Head Record

Spain and France have met 37 times, with Spain winning 16, France 13, and 8 draws. Their last meeting was in the 2024 Euros, which Spain won 2-1. This is their first World Cup knockout encounter since 2006, when France won 3-1 in the Round of 16.

🔮 Match Prediction

Spain's control and depth give them a slight edge, but France's counter-attacking threat makes them dangerous. Expect a tense, high-quality contest. Spain's ability to keep the ball could prove decisive, but Mbappé's magic can change any game. A 2-1 win for Spain after extra time is our prediction.
