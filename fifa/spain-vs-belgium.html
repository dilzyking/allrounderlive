Spain vs Belgium – Quarter-final Clash

📅 10 July 2026 📍 MetLife Stadium, New Jersey ⏱️ 12:30 IST 🏆 FIFA World Cup 2026 – Quarter-final

Spain and Belgium face off in a blockbuster quarter-final at MetLife Stadium, with a semi-final berth on the line. La Roja, the 2010 champions, have been in scintillating form, while Belgium's 'Golden Generation' are desperate to finally deliver a major trophy. This promises to be a tactical masterclass between two of Europe's finest footballing nations.

Spain's Road to the Quarters

Luis de la Fuente's side topped Group B with a perfect nine points, scoring ten goals and conceding just one. Their 4–0 demolition of Croatia and a 3–1 victory over Italy showcased their attacking fluidity and defensive solidity. Rodri has been the heartbeat of the team, controlling games from deep, while Álvaro Morata leads the line with four goals. The emergence of Lamine Yamal has given Spain a new dimension on the right flank, and the midfield trio of Pedri, Gavi, and Fabián Ruiz has provided endless creativity.

Belgium's Resurgence

Domenico Tedesco's Belgium finished second in Group E behind Portugal, but their performances have been impressive. A 3–2 win over Denmark and a 2–0 victory against Canada showed their attacking depth, while a narrow 1–0 loss to Portugal was a wake-up call. Kevin De Bruyne remains the world-class orchestrator, and Romelu Lukaku has rediscovered his goalscoring touch with three strikes. The defensive partnership of Jan Vertonghen and Toby Alderweireld brings experience, while Thibaut Courtois is arguably the best goalkeeper in the tournament.

Tactical Breakdown

Spain will dominate possession with their trademark tiki-taka, using the width provided by Yamal and Nico Williams to stretch the Belgian defence. Rodri will drop between the centre-backs to build play, while Pedri and Gavi will look to penetrate the half-spaces. Belgium, meanwhile, will sit in a compact 4-4-2 or 4-3-3, looking to hit on the counter through the pace of De Bruyne, Lukaku, and Doku. The midfield battle between Rodri and De Bruyne will be the key tactical duel of the match.

  • Rodri vs De Bruyne: The two midfield titans will dictate the tempo. Rodri's positioning and passing against De Bruyne's driving runs and creativity is a mouth-watering duel.
  • Yamal vs Theate: Spain's teenage sensation against Belgium's left-back. Yamal's dribbling and cutting inside will be a major threat.
  • Morata vs Vertonghen: The veteran Belgian defender's experience against Morata's movement and aerial ability will be crucial.

Head-to-Head Record

Spain and Belgium have met 8 times, with Spain winning 5, Belgium 2, and 1 draw. Their last encounter was a friendly in 2023, which Spain won 3–1. However, this is their first World Cup knockout meeting – the stakes are higher than ever.

🔮 Match Prediction

Spain's control and depth give them a slight edge, but Belgium's counter-attacking threat and individual brilliance make them dangerous. Expect a tense, high-quality contest. Spain's ability to keep the ball and create chances should see them through, but it could go to extra time. A 2–1 win for Spain after 90 minutes is our prediction.
