Argentina vs Egypt – Round of 16 Showdown

📅 7 July 2026 📍 MetLife Stadium, New Jersey ⏱️ 12:30 IST 🏆 FIFA World Cup 2026 – Round of 16

Argentina and Egypt lock horns in a blockbuster Round of 16 clash at MetLife Stadium, with both sides dreaming of a quarter-final berth. La Albiceleste, the reigning world champions, come into this knockout tie as heavy favourites, but Egypt – led by the talismanic Mohamed Salah – have shown they are no pushovers, having topped their group in emphatic fashion.

Argentina’s Road to the Knockouts

Lionel Scaloni's men sailed through Group C with three wins out of three, scoring eight goals and conceding just once. Their 3–0 demolition of Nigeria and a 2–1 comeback victory over Saudi Arabia underlined their resilience and attacking firepower. Lionel Messi, in what is likely his final World Cup appearance, has been in sublime form, contributing three goals and two assists already. The midfield trio of Enzo Fernández, Alexis Mac Allister and Rodrigo De Paul has provided the perfect balance of creativity and steel, while the defence – marshalled by Cristian Romero and Nicolás Otamendi – has looked largely unbreachable.

Egypt’s Historic Campaign

Egypt have been one of the surprise packages of this World Cup. Under the guidance of Rui Vitória, the Pharaohs topped Group D ahead of Belgium and Japan, with a perfect record of nine points. Their 2–1 victory over Belgium – courtesy of a Salah brace – sent shockwaves through the tournament. Beyond Salah, Egypt boast a cohesive unit with Mahmoud Trezeguet providing width and creativity, and a solid defensive core led by Ahmed Hegazi and Ali Gabr. Goalkeeper Mohamed El-Shenawy has also been outstanding, keeping two clean sheets.

Tactical Breakdown

Argentina are expected to dominate possession, with Scaloni deploying his trademark 4-3-3 system. The full-backs – Nahuel Molina and Marcos Acuña – will push high to provide width, while Messi will drift infield from the right to overload the central areas. Egypt, meanwhile, will sit deep and look to hit on the counter, with Salah's pace and Trezeguet's direct running the primary weapons. The midfield battle between De Paul and Egypt's Mohamed Elneny will be crucial in dictating the tempo.

Head-to-Head Record

Argentina and Egypt have met only three times in international football, with Argentina winning twice and one draw. Their last encounter was a friendly in 2018, which Argentina won 2–0. However, this is their first meeting in a World Cup knockout fixture – the stakes couldn't be higher.

🔮 Match Prediction

Argentina's tournament experience and depth should see them through, but Egypt have the quality to make it uncomfortable. Expect a tense first half, with Argentina's quality eventually telling in the second period. A 2–1 victory for La Albiceleste seems the most likely outcome, but don't rule out Salah producing a moment of magic to force extra time.
